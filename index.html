    About E-Guard

    E-Guard is an early-stage project in the fields of HealthTech, elderly care, AI and IoT. It is designed to help older adults live more safely and independently, while keeping their families informed about their well-being.

    Our vision

    To create a safer environment for elderly individuals by combining smart technology, health monitoring and family connectivity.

    Our mission

    To reduce the risks of elderly people living alone by giving families timely information, health monitoring and emergency notifications.

    The problem

    Many elderly people spend long hours alone while relatives work or live elsewhere.

    Unnoticed emergencies

    Sudden falls or medical events may go unseen for a long time.

    No constant oversight

    Families cannot watch over a loved one around the clock.

    Manual checking

    Traditional methods rely on frequent phone calls and visits.

    Delayed response

    The later an emergency is detected, the greater the risk may be.

    Constant worry

    Without reliable information, families often feel anxious.

    Our solution

    Our proposed solution brings three components together in one connected system.

    Smart wearable bracelet

    Designed to collect health readings such as blood oxygen saturation (SpO2) and, depending on the selected sensor, blood pressure and other vital signs. Readings are sent to the app.

    In development

    AI-powered camera monitoring

    Aims to help detect falls, unusual movement or inactivity, and potential emergencies. Camera access follows defined permissions and privacy rules.

    Planned

    Flutter mobile app

    The central interface connecting elderly users, families and doctors, with a different experience for each role.

    In development

    Three roles, one app

    Elderly user

    View personal health readings, receive notifications, reach emergency help options and manage approved family connections.

    Family and guardians

    Follow health readings, receive warning and emergency notifications, and view the camera according to permissions.

    Doctors

    Review relevant medical information for linked patients and add recommendations and notes. Doctors do not access the live camera or receive fall alerts, and are not responsible for real-time emergency response.

    How it works

    Alert levels

    Normal

    The system is operating normally and no concerns are detected.

    Warning

    Readings or behavior suggest a potential concern. The elderly user and guardians are notified.

    Emergency

    A potentially serious situation is detected. The user may be asked to confirm they are okay. If they ask for help or do not respond within a defined period, the alert escalates to family members.

    E-Guard is designed to support early awareness and human intervention. It does not diagnose conditions and does not replace medical professionals or emergency services.

    Why E-Guard

    Many solutions focus on a single aspect of elderly care. E-Guard aims to bring these together:

    Health monitoring through wearable technology

    Camera-based safety monitoring

    AI-assisted detection of potential emergencies

    Family-focused notifications and communication

    Doctor-supported medical follow-up

    Our vision

    We see a future where growing older at home feels safer for the person and calmer for the family. E-Guard is our first step toward that: a connected, privacy-aware system that supports people instead of replacing them.
